# Design rationale and references

The assignment's constraints determine the architecture families. Recent research informs evaluation and experiment design; a recent paper is not evidence that its hyperparameters are optimal for these much smaller models.

- **Character GPT:** a smaller-width, longer-context decoder than the teammate's setup, with manually implemented masked multihead attention. Keep a fixed official-data selection and fit character vocabulary on training only. [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762); [TinyStories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07759); [official TinyStories dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/roneneldan/TinyStories).
- **Sentiment:** compare an order-insensitive max-pool baseline, recurrent BiLSTM, and convolutional receptive fields from dilation. The comparison matters more than tiny arbitrary parameter changes. Select on validation macro F1, retain one untouched test protocol and quantify uncertainty. [Convolutional and recurrent sequence modeling study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.01271); [Yelp Polarity data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/fancyzhx/yelp_polarity).
- **CycleGAN:** retain the original two-direction cycle/identity objective, with 9 residual blocks and higher resolution. An absolute identity coefficient of 5 here is equivalent to the original implementation's identity factor 0.5 when its cycle coefficient is 10. [CycleGAN paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.10593); [author implementation](https://github.com/junyanz/pytorch-CycleGAN-and-pix2pix).
- **Image evaluation:** report multiple measures and fixed real/fake sample counts; FID alone cannot establish content preservation or perceptual success. Use FID/KID together with LPIPS, feature cosine, cycle error and actual human ratings. [Clean-FID](https://github.com/GaParmar/clean-fid); [LPIPS author implementation](https://github.com/richzhang/PerceptualSimilarity); [PRDC author implementation](https://github.com/clovaai/generative-evaluation-prdc).

Initial settings are in `task*/srinidhi/config.json`. Only change one or a few meaningful factors after a baseline run; record every tried configuration and select using validation, not the held-out test set. Full training times and costs remain estimates until GPU rehearsal measures actual throughput.
