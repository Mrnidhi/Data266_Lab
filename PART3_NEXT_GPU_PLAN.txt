Rationale and limits

versus 1.203 on validation images. Recheck this on the selected checkpoint.
DiffAugment addresses a plausible generalization problem, but no improvement
is guaranteed. Identity 5 versus 2.5 was effectively tied in the previous study.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2006.10738v4

of transposed-convolution versus resize-convolution upsampling is justified.
